Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is LearnoCentric one platform or several tools?

One. Curriculum, lessons, assessments, worksheets, portfolios, live classes, gradebook, analytics, reporting, messaging and safeguarding are all part of the same system, with a shared login for every role.

Do we need a separate tool for live classes?

No. Live video classes are built in, including camera, mic, screen share and automatic attendance, so lessons and their recordings of who attended live alongside everything else.

Can families and learners use it too?

Yes. LearnoCentric is built around distinct experiences for school admins, teachers, learners and parents/guardians, each sees exactly what's relevant to them.

How is our data kept safe?

Every school's data is isolated to that institution, sensitive learner details are restricted to admin roles, and safeguarding is a first-class feature with severity and access controls.

How do we get started?

Request a demo and we'll walk you through LearnoCentric with your own subjects and classes, then help you get set up.
