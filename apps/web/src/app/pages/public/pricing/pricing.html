Pricing questions

How pricing works

Why isn't the price listed?

Schools vary widely in size and needs, so we price each plan to fit — rather than a one-size-fits-all number. Tell us about your school and you'll get a clear, tailored quote.

How is a plan sized?

Typically by the number of learners and the features you need. As your school grows or adopts more of the platform, your plan can grow with it.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many schools begin with Starter or Standard and move up as more of the school comes online — your data and setup carry across.

Is there help getting set up?

Every plan includes onboarding to help you add your curriculum, classes and staff. Premium adds priority onboarding and support.
