About Learnocentric

Learning, brought together.

Schools shouldn't have to stitch together a dozen tools to run teaching and learning. Learnocentric exists to put the whole cycle, from curriculum and assessment to live classes, feedback and reporting, in one coherent place.

Why we built it

One platform beats a pile of tools

When lessons live in one app, quizzes in another, video somewhere else and reports in a spreadsheet, teachers lose time and leaders lose the picture. We bring those pieces into a single system so the data connects: a quiz result, a portfolio rating and a report card are all part of the same story.

The result: less admin for teachers, a clearer path for learners, and a live view of performance for the people running the school.

What we believe

The principles behind the product

hub

One source of truth

Every role sees the same underlying data, shaped for what they need. No re-keying, no reconciling exports between systems.

insights

Real data, not busywork

Analytics are computed from what actually happened in class, so the numbers guide teaching instead of adding to the workload.

diversity_3

Every learner counts

Attention lists, interventions and competency portfolios exist so no learner quietly slips through the gaps.

verified_user

Safe by design

School data is isolated, sensitive details are restricted by role, and safeguarding is a first-class part of the platform.

Who it's for

Built for the whole school community

From school leaders and administrators to teachers, learners, and the parents and guardians who support them, Learnocentric is designed for the whole school community, working from the same platform.
